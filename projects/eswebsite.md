In mid-to-late 2025, I overhauled the [Department of Earth Sciences at UH Mānoa website](https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/earthsciences/) by migrating its content from a static, Bootstrap-based site to WordPress based on a pre-authored professional design. The transition to a CMS enabled more straightforward, dynamic content management, better scalability, and an overall up-to-date structure. Future expanded uses and improvements are planned, and both I and the department are open to feedback on the site using the form in the footer.
